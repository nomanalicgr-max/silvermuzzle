How We Research Products

We do not claim hands-on testing unless it is true. Here is what we do for every roundup and category:

  • Read owner reviews — especially long-term reviews from owners of senior dogs, and reviews that mention size, weight and durability.
  • Check materials and safety — cover fabric, foam type and thickness, grip and non-slip bases, buckles, stitching and any small parts.
  • Compare specifications — dimensions, weight limits, washability, warranty and what is actually included in the box.
  • Look for the drawback — if a bed is hard to wash, a ramp is heavy to carry, or a harness runs small, we say so.
  • Update when things change — prices, sizes and listings change. Check the listing before you buy.

We never claim a product treats, cures or prevents any condition. Wording like “designed to support comfort” means exactly that — comfort and support, not medicine.
