Memory Foam vs Orthopedic Egg-Crate Dog Beds

Both are sold as “orthopedic” beds, but they feel and last quite differently. Here is the honest difference, without the marketing.

Memory foamEgg-crate foam
How it feelsDense, even support; moulds slowly to the bodyLighter, softer, with peaks and valleys
SupportGenerally better for heavier dogs; holds shape longerComfortable at first, but can flatten faster under weight
DurabilityLasts longer if thick enough (4 inches or more)May need replacing sooner for large dogs
PriceUsually higherUsually lower
WeightHeavier to moveLighter to move and store
Best forMedium, large and giant dogs; dogs who sleep in one spotSmall dogs; lighter dogs; a spare or travel bed

Our take

For most senior dogs, memory foam is the better long-term choice — especially if your dog is medium or large. Egg-crate foam is a fair budget option for small, light dogs, or as a second bed.

Whatever the foam type, check the thickness and whether the cover is removable and washable. A thin bed of either type will flatten quickly and stop supporting your dog.
