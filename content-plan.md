## Publishing notes

- Every article and review must carry the disclaimer: "This content is for general information only and is not veterinary advice. Always consult your vet about your dog's health."
- Never claim a product treats, cures or prevents any condition. Use "designed to support comfort."
- Do not claim hands-on testing unless true. Method: reading owner reviews, checking materials and safety, comparing specifications.
- Each new article should link to one category page and one roundup/comparison where relevant, and link back from the category page's "related reading" when published.
