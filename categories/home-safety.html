Home Safety: Rugs, Traction, Gates and Night Lights

Most slips happen at home, on the floors your dog crosses every day. A few well-placed rugs and a night light can change that.

What to look for

  • Rug grip: rugs need a non-slip backing or a separate grip pad underneath, or they become a hazard themselves.
  • Clear paths: keep the route to water, the door and the bed clear of clutter and trailing cables.
  • Gates: a gate at the top of stairs is worth more than any gadget if your dog is unsteady.
  • Night lights: older eyes see less well in the dark. A low light on the hallway helps.

Common questions

Do I need the most expensive option?

Usually not. Match the size and weight limit to your dog first, then choose the best you can afford in that size. A well-fitting mid-price option beats a premium one in the wrong size.

What if my dog will not use it?

Introduce it slowly, with treats, and let your dog investigate it at their own pace. Never force them. If they still refuse after a few days, it may be the wrong shape or size — check the return policy before you buy.
